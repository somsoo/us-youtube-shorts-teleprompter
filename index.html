🧮 How the timing works

  • Time = words ÷ pace + pauses. 150 words at 150 words per minute take 60 seconds; two 1-second [pause] beats make it 62 seconds.
  • Words: groups of letters or numbers separated by spaces. Standalone symbols such as a dash are not counted, and numbers count as one word even if they take longer to say, so leave a little room.
  • Pace: about 150 words per minute is a common average for English speech. Speakers who are easy to follow range from about 120 to 165. Your own measured pace is the most accurate.
  • Teleprompter: it moves one word at a time at your pace and holds on each [pause], so reading along takes the estimated time. Speed changes apply from the current word on.

Source: Baruch College, City University of New York, Tools for Clear Speech, “Speaking Rate”: the average English speaker talks at about 150 words per minute (citing the National Center for Voice and Speech), with examples of clear speech at about 120 and 165 words per minute; speaking rate is words divided by minutes. ❓ Frequently asked questions

How is the time calculated?

Time equals the number of words divided by your pace in words per minute, plus any [pause] beats you add. For example, 150 words at 150 words per minute take 60 seconds, and two 1-second pauses make it 62 seconds. Words are counted as groups of letters or numbers separated by spaces.

What pace should I use?

About 150 words per minute is a common average for English speech. The best number is your own, so use Measure my pace: read your script aloud once, and the page works out your words per minute and uses it for the timing and the teleprompter.

How many words fit in 60 seconds?

At 150 words per minute, about 150 words fit in 60 seconds, 75 in 30 seconds and 37 in 15 seconds. At a faster 165 words per minute, about 165 words fit in 60 seconds. The table on this page updates for your pace.

How does the teleprompter keep pace?

It moves word by word at your words per minute and holds on each [pause] for the length you chose, so reading along takes the same time as the estimate. You can pause, restart, change speed while reading, make the text larger and mirror it for a teleprompter glass.

Is my script saved or uploaded?

Your script and settings are saved only in this browser so they are there next time. They are never sent to us, and share links contain only your pace and target, not your script. Use Clear script to delete it.

⚠️ Please note

Times are estimates. Real recordings vary with delivery, numbers, names and edits, so do a practice read and leave a little room under any hard time limit. Check the current length limits of the app or site you post to.
